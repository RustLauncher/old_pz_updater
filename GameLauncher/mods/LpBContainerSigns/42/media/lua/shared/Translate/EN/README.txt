If you are reading this message because you would like to contribute to translating this mod into your language, thank you!

My goal is to make this mod accessible to as many people as possible. I will therefore be happy to work with anyone who would like to improve or complete the translation of the mod in another language.

If you would like to contribute, I invite you to add me on Steam and let me know that you would like to help translate the mod. Whenever the mod is updated and the localization files need to be modified, I will contact you whenever possible so that you can update your translation.

Once I receive the updated files, I will do my best to include your work in the next update of the mod.

Everyone who contributes to the translation will be credited.

If my name is listed as the author of a translation, this means that the translation was either done by me personally or with the help of machine translators, dictionaries, or other online tools, to the best of my ability.

If you notice any mistakes, awkward phrasing, or translations that could be improved, please don't hesitate to let me know. Some parts may have been translated or completed using these tools, which can naturally result in imperfections.

Thank you to everyone who takes the time to contribute to improving the mod's translations!
