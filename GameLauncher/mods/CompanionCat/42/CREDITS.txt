CD: Cats -- Asset Credits & Licenses
====================================

Sounds
------
Cat voices: media/sound/CDCat*.ogg
Source: BigSoundBank.com (Joseph Sardin), "free and royalty free".
  Meow      -> CDCatMeow1.ogg
  Growling  -> CDCatGrowl1.ogg, CDCatGrowl2.ogg
  Hiss      -> CDCatHiss1.ogg
  Purring   -> CDCatPurr1.ogg, CDCatPurr2.ogg, CDCatPurr3.ogg, CDCatPurrLoop1.ogg
Clips cut and converted to mono 44.1 kHz Vorbis with ffmpeg, peaks normalised to
-1.4 dBFS to match the CompanionDogs clips. Credited with thanks.

Model
-----
media/models_X/Skinned/Cat_Body.glb -- rebuilt from a third-party GLB: decimated to
6,113 vertices, pruned to 56 bones to fit the engine limit, clips remapped to the
raccoon animset, root node normalised to identity.
Rebuild pipeline: our own tooling, not shipped with the mod.
