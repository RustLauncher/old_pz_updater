/*
    Fletcher: the Fletcher's Bench, a workstation for dedicated archers (shared/Fletcher/Gear.lua F.MATCHED).
    Built from the build menu like vanilla's Pottery Bench (a CraftBench entity), but hard to reach:
    Carpentry 4 and Archery 6, planks and nails, a Fletching Jig (Welding, the game's MetalWelding skill, and Carving) and a
    bench vise (vanilla's Crude Bench Vise, or a pair of Vise Grips). Its crafting window runs the
    recipes tagged FletchingBench: batches of five matched arrows / bolts, 40 percent quicker per
    arrow than by hand (Fletcher_Matched.txt, GENERATED by animation/scripts/matched_arrows.py).
    Sprites: our own tile sheet fletcher_01 (media/fletcher.tiles + texturepacks/Fletcher.pack, rendered
    by animation/scripts/art/fletching_bench.py, packed by art/build_tiles.py). A sprite can belong to
    ONE entity only: a duplicate stops the world loading.

    The Stave Rack: a bowyer's drying rack (vanilla's DryingCraftLogic, like the grain racks), the only
    place staves season (FL_SeasonBowStave / FL_SeasonLongbowStave, Tags = FletcherStaveRack). Its
    overlays show the staves on the pegs, green, part seasoned and seasoned (style Staves).
*/
