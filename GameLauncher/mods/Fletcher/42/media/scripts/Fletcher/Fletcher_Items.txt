/*
    Fletcher items: bows (Simple, Longbow, Recurve, Compound; urban: PVC, Board, Hockey Stick, Taped Recurve) and arrows (five types).

    Arrow types: each arrow is its own AmmoType (media/registries.lua). A bow's script AmmoType is
    only the default: client/Fletcher/Bow.lua points the empty bow at the arrow to nock next
    (preferred, else best carried) with setAmmoType, so vanilla reload / unload take that arrow.
    Bow and arrow numbers in the scripts are fallbacks; the live stats come from F.BOW_DEFS /
    F.ARROW_DEFS (shared/Fletcher/Gear.lua) and the Archery level.

    The bow is built on the game's ranged-weapon system for now (aiming, firing, ammo, hit chance,
    multiplayer sync come with it); our animations replace the rifle ones (AnimSets, condition
    FletcherBow), and the real server-side projectile replaces the hit resolution in phase 2.
    The bow you see is not the hand model (WeaponSprite = an invisible mesh): it's a worn piece
    skinned to the prop bones (BowVisualRest / BowVisualDraw), kept on by client/Fletcher/Bow.lua.
*/
