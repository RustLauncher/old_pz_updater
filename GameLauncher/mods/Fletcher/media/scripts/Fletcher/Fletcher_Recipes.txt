/*
    Fletcher recipes: bows (Simple, Longbow, Recurve; the Compound bow is found, not made) and
    arrows (the head decides the type: fire-hardened tip, stone, nail, broadhead; Carbon arrows
    are found, in boxes). Crossbows: a carved stock with a wooden (Hand) or steel (Lever) prod; the
    Crank Crossbow is found. Bolts like arrows: fire-hardened, iron (nail) or broadhead tips;
    Aluminum bolts are found, in boxes.
    Carving gates and trains the carving steps (like vanilla's spears), FlintKnapping the stone heads,
    Archery the bowyer and fletcher steps.
    Recipes with NeedToBeLearn + AutoLearnAny = Skill:N are taught early by a recipe magazine
    (Fletcher_Magazines.txt) down to their lower SkillRequired floor, or learned on reaching level N
    (the game checks on level-up and on world load, so characters already past N keep them).
*/
