[ 9/2/26 v1.0.0 ]
Initial release.
Makes the sheet fit the window it is hanging on. Vanilla uses the same small bedsheet on every window, so on tall windows, church windows and storefront glass it covers the top half and zombies see straight through the rest. The fitted sheet is cut to the size of the glass and covers the whole pane.
Sheets that are already hanging are refitted as the area loads, including ones you hung before installing the mod and ones the game put up on its own.
Doors keep the vanilla sheet, glass storefront doors included. A few very large windows that span more than one wall tile are left alone.
The mod does not hang sheets itself: the game adds its sheet the normal way and the mod swaps in one that fits, so open/close, refunds, barricades, saves and multiplayer sync are untouched vanilla.
