CompanionDogs: Asset Credits & Licenses
========================================

Sounds
------
Dog voices (bark / growl / whine / idle / death): media/sound/CDDog*.ogg
Source: BigSoundBank.com (Joseph Sardin), CC0 (public domain).
Attribution optional; credited with thanks.
The growl (CDDogGrowl) is made from "Growling cat" (ID 1886 / 1887),
pitch-shifted down with high/low-pass EQ into a deep dog growl.
Clips cut/converted to mono 44.1 kHz with ffmpeg.

Eating / drinking foley: media/sound/CDDogEat1.ogg, CDDogDrink1.ogg
Source: Freesound.org, CC0 (public domain).
Eating: "a dog eats his kibble" by aisselles (#725619).
Drinking: "Dog drinking water" by artemditkovsky (#860331).
Cut/converted to mono 44.1 kHz with ffmpeg. Credited with thanks.

Bite foley reuses the vanilla "ZombieBite" sound.

Models
------
German Shepherd body mesh: our own 3D model.
Re-skinned to a custom Biped skeleton and adapted for the game.

Skeleton + skinning: original work.
Rig and export pipeline: our own tooling, not shipped with the mod.

Performance reports & diagnostics
--------------------------------
Third-party add-ons whose measurements and code reading fed fixes shipped here.
No code or assets from them are included; the credit is for the diagnosis.

Lucy - "Lucy's Mod Fixes" (Workshop 3780752367).
Profiled the stray spawner's LoadChunk handler on a 255-mod dedicated server
(27.5 ms mean, 273.89 ms peak in a single call against a 100 ms tick budget),
identified the memoisation leaks, and proposed the per-tick work cap.
Also authored the read-only probe behind the duplicate-dog investigation.

genolark - "Companion Dogs - Performance Patch" (Workshop 3781402232).
Building classification caching, including reuse across map chunks.

shmoodle - "DOTD CompanionDogs Nerf" (Workshop 3777182198).
Read the two reachability flood-fills line by line and identified their real
costs: a temporary object allocated per neighbour examined, and a cache keyed
on the dog's own tile, which made a walking dog rebuild the flood every step.
The retuned values that mod ships were not adopted; the diagnosis was.

Health panel and medicines
--------------------------
Dog health panel (media/ui/CDHealth/*.png: the body silhouette and one layer per body part)
and the in-game health debug window: design and art by freylith, contributed to Companion Dogs.
The six medicine item icons (media/textures/Item_CompanionDogs*.png) and the design of the
canine flu, heartworm, skin allergy and flea and tick illnesses are hers as well.
The herbal remedies, their recipes and their 35 item icons (herbs, tinctures, remedies, baths
and the Home Vet magazine) come from her mod Man's Best Friend (Steam Workshop 3751069565).
Used with thanks.
